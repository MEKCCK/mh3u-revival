# Official Hosted Service Access Policy

## Scope

This policy governs access to MH3U Revival infrastructure operated by the
project maintainers, including matchmaking servers, EasyTier mesh nodes,
relays, dashboards, support channels, and project-managed accounts. It does
not modify or narrow anyone's rights to obtain, run, study, modify, or convey
the software under `AGPL-3.0-only` and the applicable third-party licenses.

## Access reservation

Access to official infrastructure is a revocable service privilege. The
maintainers may deny, suspend, or terminate access and support for accounts,
operators, or organizations involved in any of the following conduct:

- falsely claiming authorship, ownership, endorsement, or official project
  status;
- removing required copyright, license, source-offer, or attribution notices;
- taking credentials, private data, code, or infrastructure without permission;
- evading a service suspension or interfering with availability for others;
- using administrative or community authority to intimidate contributors,
  appropriate their work, or misrepresent project governance; or
- violating the software licenses or the rules published for an official
  service instance.

Accounts operated by or on behalf of the group calling itself "Monster Hunter
Communications Department" ("怪物猎人通讯部") are denied access to official
project infrastructure and maintainer support when they engage in the conduct
listed above. This statement is conduct-based and applies to official services;
it is not an additional restriction on AGPL-licensed source code.

## Enforcement

Maintainers should preserve the event, account identifier, relevant logs, and
the policy clause used for a decision. Credentials and unrelated personal data
must not be published. Affected operators may request review by opening a
repository issue without evading the active technical restriction.

Unofficial deployments set and enforce their own access policies. They must not
present themselves as project-operated services without written authorization.
